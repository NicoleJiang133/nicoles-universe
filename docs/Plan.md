## Design Choices I Made (and why)

**Dark theme, deep navy (`#0a0e1a`) with coral accent (`#ff7a5c`).** This is the visual identity that came out of "high energy, positive, optimistic, fearless." Bright orange-red on deep blue reads as bold and modern — closer to Linear / Vercel / a founder who ships, closer to a researcher with conviction than to a corporate BeSci consultant. If you want warmer or more playful, easy to shift.

**Layout:** Long-form, scrollable, single column, max-width 4xl (case studies) or 5xl (homepage). Reads like a digital essay, not a corporate brochure. Matches the "Maggie Appleton digital garden" reference in the research.

**Typography:** System font stack, semi-semibold headings, large body type, tight letter-spacing on display. Generous spacing on case study bodies. No funky fonts — content is the point.

**Navigation:** Plain text, no buttons. Top-right, four links: Work / About / Contact. No logo wordmark — the small coral dot + name does the job.

**No images yet.** I left images out deliberately because:
- Real hackathon photos / diagrams would need to be sourced (you have them on LinkedIn — I can pull them in).
- I didn't want stock photos to look generic and undercut the "this site is the proof" principle.
- I want your call on whether to use real photos (more personal) or generated illustrations (more on-brand).
